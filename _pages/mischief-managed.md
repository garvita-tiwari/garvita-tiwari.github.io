    "After all this time? Always."

They say the Potterhead kid in you never truly grows up; they just get a passport and some visa stamps and a better sense of direction. This is a collection of my real-world travels to find the magic I first discovered in books. Whether I'm standing on Platform 9¾ or wandering through the Scottish Highlands, these journeys remind me that Hogwarts will always be there to welcome you home.
